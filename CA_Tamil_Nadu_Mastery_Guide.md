## 1. The Three Student Groups / Levels in ICAI Chartered Accountancy (New Scheme)

Under the **ICAI New Scheme of Education and Training**, students enter and progress through **three distinct levels (groups)** depending on whether they join right after **Class 12 (Samacheer Kalvi / CBSE)** or via the **Direct Entry Route after Graduation**:

| Level / Student Group | Who Takes It & Entry Route | Papers & Structure | Marking, Negative Marking & Passing Rule |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Group / Level 1: CA Foundation** *(Tri-annual: Jan / May / Sep)* | **Class 12 Students / Pass-outs** (Samacheer Kalvi Commerce or Science stream; can register after Class 10 and appear after Class 12 board exams). | **4 Papers (400 Marks Total):**<br>• **Paper 1:** Accounting *(100M — Descriptive)*<br>• **Paper 2:** Business Laws *(100M — Descriptive: Contract Act, Sale of Goods, Partnership, LLP, Companies Act, NI Act)*<br>• **Paper 3:** Quantitative Aptitude *(100M — MCQ: Business Mathematics 40M, Logical Reasoning 20M, Statistics 40M)*<br>• **Paper 4:** Business Economics *(100M — MCQ)* | • **Papers 1 & 2:** Subjective (`0` negative marking)<br>• **Papers 3 & 4:** Objective MCQ (**`+1` Correct, `-0.25` Negative Marking**)<br>• **Passing Rule:** Minimum **40% (40/100)** in each of the 4 papers **AND 50% Aggregate (200/400)** across all 4 papers in one sitting. |
| **Group / Level 2: CA Intermediate (Group I & Group II)** *(Tri-annual: Jan / May / Sep)* | **1. Foundation Route:** Cleared CA Foundation.<br>**2. Direct Entry Route:** Commerce Graduates/Post-Graduates with **≥55% marks** (or Non-Commerce Graduates with **≥60% marks**, or CS/CMA Intermediate pass) — **exempt from CA Foundation!** | **6 Papers across 2 Groups (600 Marks Total):**<br>**Group I (300M):**<br>• **Paper 1:** Advanced Accounting *(AS & Company Accounts)*<br>• **Paper 2:** Corporate and Other Laws *(Companies Act 2013 Sec 1–148, Foreign Cos, LLP, General Clauses, FEMA)*<br>• **Paper 3:** Taxation *(Sec A: Income-tax Law 50M + Sec B: GST 50M)*<br>**Group II (300M):**<br>• **Paper 4:** Cost and Management Accounting *(100M)*<br>• **Paper 5:** Auditing and Ethics *(Standards on Auditing SAs, CARO 2020)*<br>• **Paper 6:** Financial Management (50M) & Strategic Management (50M) | • **Every Paper (1 to 6):** **30 Marks Case-Scenario MCQs** (`+1` or `+2`, **`0` Negative Marking!**) + **70 Marks Descriptive/Computation**.<br>• **Passing Rule:** **40% per paper** + **50% Group Aggregate** (`150/300` for 1 group or `300/600` for both, with set-off if appearing both together).<br>• **60%+ Exemption Rule:** Scoring **≥60** in any paper grants a **3-Attempt Exemption** (and can be made permanent under New Scheme rules). |
| **Group / Level 3: CA Final (Group I & II) + Mandatory SPOM (Sets A, B, C, D)** *(Bi-annual: May / Nov)* | **Advanced / Articleship Students:** Cleared **both groups of CA Intermediate**, completed **2 Years of Practical Training (Articleship)** + **AICITSS (Advanced ICITSS)**, and cleared **SPOM (Self-Paced Online Modules Sets A, B, C & D)** before appearing in CA Final. | **SPOM (Computer-Based MCQ, 100M each, 50% Pass, 0 Neg):**<br>• **Set A:** Corporate & Economic Laws *(IBC 2016, SEBI, FEMA, PMLA)*<br>• **Set B:** Strategic Cost Management & Performance Evaluation *(SCMPE)*<br>• **Set C & Set D:** Elective & Multidisciplinary Modules<br>**CA Final (6 Papers, 600 Marks):**<br>**Group I:**<br>• **Paper 1:** Financial Reporting *(FR — Ind AS & CFS)*<br>• **Paper 2:** Advanced Financial Management *(AFM)*<br>• **Paper 3:** Advanced Auditing, Assurance & Professional Ethics<br>**Group II:**<br>• **Paper 4:** Direct Tax Laws & International Taxation<br>• **Paper 5:** Indirect Tax Laws *(GST 75M + Customs & FTP 25M)*<br>• **Paper 6:** Integrated Business Solutions *(IBS — 100M Open-Book Multidisciplinary Case Study)* | • **Every Final Paper (1 to 6):** **30% Case-Scenario MCQs** (`0` Negative Marking) + **70% Descriptive**.<br>• **Paper 6 (IBS):** **4 Hours Open-Book** (5 Case Studies of 25M each, attempt any 4; each has 10M MCQs + 15M Descriptive across FR, AFM, Audit, DT, IDT, Law & SCMPE).<br>• **Passing Rule:** **40% per paper** + **50% Group Aggregate** + **60%+ Exemption Rule**. |

## 2. Public / Government Free CA Coaching Institutes & Welfare Schemes in Tamil Nadu

Students in Tamil Nadu who cannot afford private coaching fees (`₹40,000–₹1,20,000`) have access to **official Government of Tamil Nadu and ICAI Statutory Public Coaching & Scholarship programs**:

### A. TAHDCO (Tamil Nadu Adi Dravidar Housing & Development Corporation — Govt. of Tamil Nadu)
- **Portal:** [tahdco.com](https://tahdco.com) / [iei.tahdco.com](https://iei.tahdco.com)
- **What It Offers:** **100% Free Coaching + Course Fee Support** for **CA Foundation & CA Intermediate** (alongside CS and CMA) for eligible SC/ST students from Tamil Nadu whose annual family income is within the Government ceiling (`≤ ₹2.5–3.0 Lakhs`).
- **Institutional Tie-ups:** TAHDCO partners with premier Chennai/TN coaching academies and residential facilities so students receive full study materials, mock test series, and mentorship at zero cost.

### B. Naan Mudhalvan Scheme (Tamil Nadu Skill Development Corporation — TNSDC)
- **Portal:** [naanmudhalvan.tn.gov.in](https://www.naanmudhalvan.tn.gov.in)
- **What It Offers:** Flagship Government of Tamil Nadu initiative across Government & Government-Aided Arts, Science & Commerce Colleges (B.Com, BBA, BA Economics). Provides BFSI (Banking, Financial Services & Insurance), Corporate Accounting, GST, Tally/ERP, Audit readiness, and competitive professional exam tracks bridging college commerce students into the **CA Direct Entry Route**.

### C. ICAI Board of Studies (BoS) Free Live Learning Classes & SSP Scholarships (`icai.org`)
- **Portals:** [boslive.icai.org](https://boslive.icai.org) | [ssp.icai.org](https://ssp.icai.org) | **ICAI BoS Mobile App** | [ICAI CA Tube (Official YouTube)](https://www.youtube.com/@icai_ca_tube)
- **What It Offers:**
  1. **100% Free Live Virtual Classes:** Taught by ICAI empanelled top national faculty for every single paper of **CA Foundation, CA Intermediate, and CA Final**, with downloadable PDF notes, RTPs (Revision Test Papers), MTPs (Mock Test Papers), and Case Scenario booklets.
  2. **ICAI SSP Need-Based & Merit Scholarships:**
     - **Need-Based (Economically Weaker Students):** Open to CA Foundation, Intermediate, and Final students whose parents' annual income is `≤ ₹3,00,000` (up to `₹5,00,000` under revised slabs), paying **`₹1,500 to ₹3,000/month`** directly to the student's bank account via SSP (`ssp.icai.org`).
     - **Merit-cum-Need & Ranker Scholarships:** **`₹2,500 to ₹5,000/month`** for students securing exemptions/ranks.
     - **75% Registration Fee Waiver** for students from economically weaker sections and wards of deceased members/martyrs.

### D. Charitable Trusts & Subsidized Public CA Coaching in Chennai & Tamil Nadu
- **Anandam Charitable Trust (Chennai — Ambattur / T. Nagar):** Provides **100% Free CA Foundation & Intermediate Coaching**, books, ICAI registration fee sponsorship, and mentorship for meritorious economically disadvantaged students from Tamil Nadu Government & Corporation schools ([anandamtrust.org](https://anandamtrust.org)).
- **Ramanathan Public Charitable Trust (RPCT, Chennai):** Offers free/heavily subsidized CA coaching batches and study hall access for underprivileged commerce students in Chennai.
- **SIRC of ICAI Regional & District Branch Oral Coaching Classes:** All 12 Tamil Nadu branches of SIRC conduct low-cost official ICAI Oral Coaching, Mock Test Series (Physical & Virtual), Reading Rooms/Libraries (`₹500–₹1,500`), and mandatory **ICITSS / AICITSS (IT & Orientation / MCS & Adv. IT)** batches.
