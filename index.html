1. The 3 Student Groups / Levels in ICAI Chartered Accountancy (New Scheme 2024–2026)

Under the ICAI New Scheme of Education and Training, students preparing for Chartered Accountancy fall into three distinct student groups/levels. Every level requires minimum 40% marks in each individual paper AND minimum 50% aggregate marks across the Group. Scoring 60% or more in any paper earns an automatic 3-Attempt Exemption in that paper!

Student Group / Level Who Takes It & Entry Route Papers & Syllabus Structure Exam Pattern, Negative Marking & Passing Criteria
Group / Level 1:
CA Foundation
(Jan / May / Sep — 3x/Year)		 Class 12 Students & Pass-outs (Samacheer Kalvi Commerce or Science stream, CBSE, ISC). Provisional registration allowed after Class 10. 4 Papers (400 Marks):
• Paper 1: Accounting (100M)
• Paper 2: Business Laws (100M — Contract, Sale of Goods, Partnership, LLP, Companies Act, NI Act)
• Paper 3: Quantitative Aptitude — Maths, LR & Statistics (100M)
• Paper 4: Business Economics (100M) 		• Papers 1 & 2: Descriptive (0 Negative)
• Papers 3 & 4: 100% MCQ with +1 Correct & -0.25 Negative Marking
• Pass Rule: 40%+ in each paper AND 50%+ Aggregate (200/400).
Group / Level 2:
CA Intermediate
(Group I & Group II)
(Jan / May / Sep — 3x/Year)		 Route A (Foundation Route): Cleared CA Foundation.
Route B (Direct Entry): Commerce Graduates/PG with ≥55% (Non-Commerce Graduates with ≥60%, or CS/CMA Inter pass) — 100% Exempt from CA Foundation! 		6 Papers across 2 Groups (600 Marks):
Group I (300M):
• P1: Advanced Accounting (AS & Co. Accounts)
• P2: Corporate & Other Laws (Companies Act Sec 1–148, LLP, FEMA)
• P3: Taxation (Income Tax 50M + GST 50M)
Group II (300M):
• P4: Cost & Management Accounting
• P5: Auditing & Ethics (SAs, CARO 2020)
• P6: Financial Mgmt (50M) & Strategic Mgmt (50M) 		• All 6 Papers: 30% Compulsory Case-Scenario MCQs (0 Negative Marking!) + 70% Descriptive.
• Pass Rule: 40%+ per paper + 50%+ Group Aggregate (150/300 per group or 300/600 both groups with set-off).
• Exemption: 60%+ in any paper exempts that paper for 3 attempts!
Group / Level 3:
CA Final (Group I & II)
+ Mandatory SPOM (Sets A–D)
(May / Nov — 2x/Year)		 Articleship & Final Students: Cleared both groups of CA Inter, completed 2 Years Practical Articleship + AICITSS, and cleared Self-Paced Online Modules (SPOM Sets A, B, C & D). SPOM (CBT Online MCQ, 100M each):
• Set A: Corporate & Economic Laws (IBC, SEBI, FEMA, PMLA)
• Set B: Strategic Cost Mgmt (SCMPE)
• Sets C & D: Elective & Multidisciplinary
CA Final (6 Papers, 600M):
• G1: P1 Financial Reporting (Ind AS), P2 Adv. Financial Mgmt (AFM), P3 Adv. Auditing & Ethics
• G2: P4 Direct Tax & Intl Tax, P5 Indirect Tax (GST & Customs), P6 Integrated Business Solutions (IBS — Open Book) 		• SPOM Sets A–D: 100M Computer-Based MCQ, 50% to Pass, 0 Negative Marking.
• Final Papers 1–6: 30% Case-Scenario MCQs (0 Negative) + 70% Descriptive.
• Paper 6 (IBS): 4-Hour Open-Book Multidisciplinary Case Study (40% MCQ + 60% Descriptive).
